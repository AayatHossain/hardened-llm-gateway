# Baseline

Each model on the 700 test items of the eval set (fingerprint `047f881e4b3b`: 161 facts, 161 maths, 161 code, 161 reasoning, 56 chat), sent through the gateway.

Scores run from 0 to 1. Pass/fail tasks score 1 per pass; chat items score the judge's rating out of 10. Brackets are 95% confidence intervals. Overlapping intervals do not mean 2 models are equal: compare their runs item by item with `evals.stats` for that.

Judged items (facts without a numeric answer, and chat) were graded by groq/openai/gpt-oss-120b (prompt version 1). How often the judge agrees with a person is measured in [calibration/RESULTS.md](calibration/RESULTS.md).

**The runs used different token limits (1000, 4096).** A lower limit cuts off more replies, so those models' scores may be lower than they would be with a higher one.

## Cost and speed

| Model | List price per 1,000 items | Tokens per item (in / out) | Median latency | Cut off | Errors | Ungraded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| groq/openai/gpt-oss-20b | $0.24 | 129 / 778 | 0.8 s | 75 | 0 | 0 |
| groq/qwen/qwen3.8-27b | $0.76 | 73 / 176 | 0.5 s | 19 | 0 | 0 |

- **List price:** what the replies would cost at the provider's paid prices (`prices.toml`); the runs themselves used free tiers. It leaves out the judge.
- **Cut off:** replies that hit the token limit, often a reasoning model that never got to answer. Judged items cut off fail without a verdict, since the judge rates unfinished replies as if they were whole; other tasks grade them as they are.
- **Errors:** items the model or provider failed to answer; they score 0.
- **Ungraded:** items the judge could not grade; they are left out of the score.
